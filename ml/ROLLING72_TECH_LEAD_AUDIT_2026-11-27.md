# Rolling72 technical-lead audit — 27 November 2026

## Decision

**No.** Current work shows a promising public-availability signal, but does not yet establish reliable MAE reduction on independent fixtures or support consequential club decisions. It does not address the separate attendance-MAE goal near 1,200 seats, and it cannot establish sales, demand, revenue, or intervention effects. Keep `live_adjustment=0`.

## Evidence that changes the decision

- **E5 / fixed drift24:** the latest saved E5 diagnosis (27 Nov, 11:35 UTC) reports event-balanced availability MAE for PERS vs DRIFT24 of **226.21 vs 85.62 (+24), 419.19 vs 150.41 (+48), and 605.68 vs 310.42 (+72)**. These are 678/496/307 repeated-origin labels from only **two exposed fixtures**; they are not independent event samples. Membership continuity is `UNKNOWN`. Almost all large +72 error is concentrated in one high-inventory fixture; its DRIFT24 signed bias is +585.25 in the stable-*sector-count* stratum. Sector-count stability is not membership continuity.
- **Fresh paired comparator:** the 27 Nov execution plan reports 99 paired bundles for one fixture, 95 scope-mismatch PERS fallbacks, four drift-ready bundles, and only three accepted +24 labels. Both methods have MAE 138.67 on those three labels; +48/+72 have no accepted labels. The fallback protects against unsupported slope extrapolation, but leaves little evidence about supported drift.
- **Damped trend:** the fixed `phi=0.80` protocol is proposed, not activated, and has no outcomes. An offline three-method prototype and six synthetic tests exist; they do not establish runtime parity or accuracy. Damping reduces long-horizon slope contribution. It may help overreaction, but cannot be presumed to fix the observed high-inventory +72 overprediction, where attenuating a downward slope could worsen the overshoot.
- **Cohort and product data:** the next cohort plan correctly requires 30 new independent +72 outcomes across at least eight home clubs. Current intake evidence reports **zero ready admissions** for the December shortlist; two named Roboticket offers still need current duplicate/admission checks and committed registration, while two mapped fixtures are Stellis and incompatible with the pinned Roboticket lane. The club aggregate contract is defined, but no accepted sales/refund/pool/membership/scan/final-attendance feed is evidenced here.

Thus, the current work is enough to justify bounded prospective research, not to claim the goal is met or to steer price, promotion, or advertising. Public available inventory can support an inventory-path decision only after its prospective error and coverage are established; it is not a sales or attendance proxy.

- **Before any damped shadow issue:** freeze protocol, implementation and evaluator hashes; pass isolated PostgreSQL/runtime parity review for shared issue clocks, exact target IDs, all fallback/refusal states, latest-invalid/no-backtracking, valid scope changes, and coverage denominators. Activate only as a separate future-only lane after review; it must not modify PERS, DRIFT24, E5, or production behavior.
- **Availability decision gate:** at least **30 new independent fixtures / 8 home clubs with accepted paired +72 labels**. Compare full-policy damped trend (including PERS fallbacks) with PERS on the exact same target set. Require at least **10% lower event-balanced +72 MAE**, a 10,000-resample paired event bootstrap (seed 42) 95% interval wholly below zero, and no worse +24 or +48 point MAE. Report DRIFT24 incremental error separately, alongside support/fallback and forecast/scoring coverage. Below floor is partial evidence; any failed criterion is not supported. This is a review gate, not a guarantee of club-level precision.
- **Commercial use gate:** first demonstrate the relevant target. Availability passes do not authorize sales, demand, final-attendance, revenue, or price/promotion claims. Accept club data only after event-time/revision and unknown-vs-zero validation, source-definition review, and sales/refund reconciliation within the documented tolerance; evaluate sales and authoritative final attendance separately with event-balanced MAE, coverage, and lead-time/decision records. Test causal intervention effects under a separately planned control design. Production activation requires a separate decision after those target-specific prospective results; no automatic promotion.
